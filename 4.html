Chocolate Cake Recipe

Ingredients

  • 2 cups All-purpose flour
  • 1½ cups Sugar
  • 1 cup Milk
  • ½ cup Cocoa powder
  • 2 Eggs
  • 1 tsp Baking powder

Instructions

  1. Preheat the oven to 180°C.
  2. Mix flour, sugar and cocoa powder in a bowl.
  3. Add eggs and milk. Mix well.
  4. Pour the mixture into a baking pan.
  5. Bake for 30 minutes.
  6. Let it cool and serve.
